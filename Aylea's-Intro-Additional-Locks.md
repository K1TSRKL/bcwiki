The game contains a lot of locks.  Locks can be attached to most restraints, to alter them in many ways.  I'll start off by talking about locks, in general.  

Locks can't be used by themselves.  Instead, they get applied to other restraints.  (To chain, for example.)  

In addition, just like I described for keys, earlier, locks can't be used by other players.  

Say you're a new player, who owns nothing.  But I own both chain, and a lock.  Then you can use the chain on me.  (Players can use items which the other person owns.)  But you can't put my lock on my chain, you have to own a lock, yourself.  (In fact, for the locks that have keys, you have to own the lock and the key, before you can use a lock.)  

This also means that to put a locked restraint on somebody requires at least two steps.  One to apply the restraint.  Then one to put the lock on the restraint.  

* The game recently added "crafted restraints", which allow the creation of restraints which have locks built in.  And these restraints allow the application of a locked restraint, in one step.  But I'm not covering crafted restraints here.  In this page, I'm pretending they don't exist.  

## Examples of using locks.  

I'm going to do some examples, to show how locks can be applied, and removed.  I'm going to use two disposable characters I created. 

* "Newby" is a brand new character, who owns no game objects at all.  
* "Redshirt" owns most of the locks and keys.  

I'm going to put leather collars on their necks, and lock them.  To start with, I'm going to use the game's basic lock, the metal padlock.  

I'll put a Leather collar on Redshirt.  Then, I will click on Redshirt again, and click on the neck zone.  (The zone in the center of the three neck zones, is where collars go.)  

When I go there, the game will show the leather collar, that Redshirt is wearing.  (It will also show all of the other collars that I *could* apply to Redshirt, instead.  But I'm not going to.)  

And, at the top of the screen, there will be a lock icon.  (If you, yourself, don't own any locks, you won't see that icon until you buy one.)  

(Screenshot showing lock icon)

Clicking that icon, will show the locks that I could apply to Redshirt's collar.  Clicking on "Metal padlock" will apply that lock to the collar.  

And I'll do the same things to Newby.  Both of them now are wearing leather collars, with a metal padlock applied.  

If Newby clicks on herself, and clicks on her neck, this is what she will see:  

(screenshot of Newby with locked on collar).  

Now, before the lock was applied, Newby could have simply used the X button to remove the collar.  After all, her hands are free.  She can just unbuckle it.  

But with the lock applied, she doesn't have the X button any more.   She can't just unfasten the collar.  She can try to "struggle out".  (Although the lock will make struggling out a lot tougher, too.)  She might not be able to struggle out of it, either.  

The lock makes the restraint permanent.  (In most cases.)  

Newby does, however, have a button to access the "lock menu".  Clicking it will show her this screen:  

(Screenshot of Newby's lock menu).  

She has the ability to "inspect the lock".  If she does so, it will tell her that it's a metal padlock, and that it was applied by me.  

Now, though.  Redshirt owns the metal padlock key.  (Redshirt read Aylea's Intro.  And Chapter 5 told her to buy the key.)  

If Redshirt goes to *her* lock menu, there will be a button there to unlock the lock.  

(Screenshot of Redshirt's lock menu)

Clicking the unlock button will unlock the lock.  (Removing it).  But the unlock button will leave the collar in place.  

(If Redshirt's hands were bound, then she would be unable to *reach* her key.  And the game would treat her as though she didn't own the key.)

Also.  Since Redshirt's collar is locked with a metal padlock, and Redshirt has the key for the lock, then when Redshirt clicks on her own collar, she *will* have the "X" button to "remove restraint".  The X button will unlock the collar, and remove the collar, in one step.  

_Some_ locks are more complicated than the basic metal padlock.  But I'll explain the ways they're different, when I describe individual locks.  

## Metal Padlock

The basic lock in the game.  It can be unlocked by anybody who owns the metal padlock key.  And almost everybody owns the key.  So it doesn't get used much.  

## Exclusive Padlock

A frequently used lock.  Might be a good candidate for "first lock to buy".  The exclusive lock can be opened, no key needed, by anybody in the game *except* the person it's locked on.  

Yes, you can use it on yourself.  And then everybody in the game can unlock you, except you.  

## Five minute lock

The five minute lock is a timer lock.  (There are other, more complicated, timer locks, that have characteristics in common with this one.  But this iis the basic one.)  

This is what you see is you "inspect" a five minute lock.  

(Screenshot of Redshirt inspecting five minute lock)

The five minute lock does not have a key.  _No one in the game can unlock the five minute lock_.  (There's no "unlock" button on the lock menu.)  Instead, when the lock is applied, it starts a five minute countdown.  When the timer gets to zero, the lock unlocks itself.  

Notice the lock options on the lock, though.  There's a box that can be checked.  If it's checked, then when the lock unlocks itself, the restraint goes away, too.  If the box is not checked, then when the timer runs out, the lock goes away, but the restraint stays.  

(The screenshot shows *me*, looking at Redshirt's lock.  I'm the person who applied the lock.  Other people looking at the lock would not have the option to check or uncheck that box.)  

And notice that "reset" button.  

_Everybody in the room has the ability to push that reset button.  Pushing it makes the five minute timer start over again._

The five minute lock doesn't get used often.  But it can be a lot of fun in the right room, with everybody watching that timer, wondering if somebody is going to push that button or not.  

## Combination Lock

The combination lock doesn't have a key.  It has a combination.  If Redshirt inspects her combination lock, this is the screen she will see.  

(Screenshot of Redshirt's combo lock).  

Redshirt can enter a combination, which is always four digits.  And click the "unlock" button.  If it's the right combination, the lock will unlock.  (If it's wrong, the lock stays locked, and the game will announce what code you tried to use.)  

She can also enter the correct code in the first box, and a different code in the second, and click the "change" button, to change the combination.  

All combination locks, when they're first applied, have the code "0000".  So, applying a restraint with a combination lock takes three steps:  

* Apply the restraint
* Go back to the restraint, and apply the combination lock.  
* Go back to the lock, inspect the lock, and change the code to something else.  

There are also three people whom the game will *tell* the combination to.  (If they inspect the lock, the code will be displayed in the box.  They still have to type in the code, or the game will act like they left the code blank.  But the game tells them which code to type.)  Those people are:  

1. The person who applied the lock.  
2. The owner (if any) of the person it's locked on.  
3. The lover(s) (if any) of the person it's locked on.  (If they own the lover's key.)  

## Password Lock

The password lock uses the same rules as the combination lock, except instead of a four digit code, it takes 1-8 letters.  (And the person applying the lock is allowed to provide a password hint, on the lock.)  

## Safeword Lock

The safeword lock works just like a password lock, except it adds a fourth person who the game will show the password to.  

4.  The person it's locked on. 

I've used it, sometimes.  When I wanted to make sure that the person could unlock themselves.  I've also seen it used for things like locking a chastity on somebody, so that *nobody else* can remove the chastity and put a lock on that the person can't remove.  

## Password Timer Lock

Same rules as the password lock, but it *also* has a timer, which can be set up to 4 hours.  When the time runs out, the lock removes itself.  (And, if the box is checked, the restraint might or might not go away at the same time.)  

## Intricate padlock

There's no key you can buy for this lock.  

Three people are treated as though they have keys for this lock.  

1. The person who applied the lock
2. The owner (if any) of the person it's locked on.  
3. The lover(s) (if any) of the person it's locked on.  (If they own the lover's key.)  

Other people are allowed to attempt to "pick the lock".  If they own the lockpick set.  Or if someone lends them some lockpicks.  

There's a mini-game involved, in picking the lock.  I'm going to write a sub-page that goes over how to pick locks.  It's a pain.  But you'll get better at it.  

In addition, the game will allow you to "give your keys to another player".  

I could put intricate padlocks on Redshirt,  Then click on Newby.  Click on "Character actions", and "Give my keys to Newby".  

If I do that, then the game will go over all of the locks that I've applied to people in the room (which might be more than just Redshirt), and change the "who applied the lock" to Newby's user number.  

After I do that, then Newby counts as "the person who applied the lock".  And I don't, any more.  

## High Security Padlock

Uses the same rules as the intricate padlock.  It's just harder to pick.  

And, it has a list of authorized users.  This allows the lock owner to "give keys to other players", by adding the other player's member numbers to the list.  The other players will be able to open the lock without picking it, too.  

## Mistress Locks

Mistress locks can be unlocked by anybody who has the mistress key.  But, that key can't be bought.  Instead, people who achieve the status of "Club Mistress" are given the key, and the locks.  (And if they lose that status, they get taken away.)  

## Pandora's Box Lock.

The lock, and the key, can't be bought.  You can earn them in the club's "Infiltration game", in the main hall.  

## Lover's Locks

Can only be unlocked by lovers of the person it's locked on, and only if they own the lover's key.  

## Owner's Locks.  

Same as "Lovers", but for owners.  The "Slave Collar", and "Love Chastity Belt" are treated as though they have owner locks already on them.
