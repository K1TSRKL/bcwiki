Chatrooms are where the majority of the multiplayer fun of Bondage Club happens. Creating a new room is a fairly straightforward process:

### Left Column of Options

Room Name: The name of the room as it is listed on the chatroom lineup. Please note that no special characters are permitted in this input.

Language: The primary language in this room.

Size: The number of players permitted in the room

Description: A short description of the room. This is listed underneath the room name, and can give a brief description of what you're looking for.

Administrator List: The player IDs permitted to use Administrative Actions in this room. By default it is assigned to the creator of the room, but you can click the Add Owner and Add Lovers buttons respectively to quickly add your owners and lovers, or you can add entries manually by player ID, separated by commas.

Ban List: Player IDs that are banned from this room. Banned users cannot see your room. Similar to the Administrator List, this list can be comma separated. Under your personal settings, in the Online section, you can opt to have any players on your Ghostlist or Blacklist automatically banned when you create a room.

### Right Column of Options

Scene: The menu in this screenshot labelled "Introduction" is the room background chosen for your room. You can navigate left and right, or select the settings button to the right to chose from all of the available backgrounds. Background images do not impact the chatroom proper, but help to "set the scene".

Block Categories: This allows you to restrict certain classifications of items or behaviors as you see fit. The list of categories is as follows:
1. Adult Baby Diaper Lovers - Prohibits ABDL items like bottles, diapers, and the like. These items will not show up as options to use on yourself or other players.
2. Futuristic / Sci-Fi - Prohibits the Futuristic series of restraints and the medical injector props.
3. Fantasy Items - Prohibits slimes, tentacles, and web restraints and the rainbow wand props.
4. Player Leashing - If a player is wearing a leash, this prevents other players from grabbing the leashing and removing the leash-wearer from your room.
5. Photographs - Prohibits the taking of photographs.
6. Arousal Activities - Prohibits Arousal Activities (actions from the lips icon in the player menu).

Game Mode: The menu in this screenshot labelled "No Game" is the game activity in running in this room. Specifics about these activities will be covered in future articles. Valid choices include:
1. Club Card
2. LARP
3. Magic Battle
4. GGTS
5. No Game

Customization: This screen allows you to pull content from external providers. Specifically you can pick background images that aren't included in the scene library, background mp3 music, and place a color filter over your chosen background.

Map Mode: The menu in this screenshot labelled "Don't Use Maps" indicates if this is a traditional chat room, a map room, or a hybrid room.
1. Don't User Maps: This is a traditional chat room. Players avatars will be displayed on the left, and the chat room displayed on the right.
2. Allow On and Off: This is a hybrid room. Players may choose to switch between either mode at will.
3. Only use maps: This is a map room. The room map will be displayed on the left, and the chat room displayed on the right. More details on how to setup a map room will be covered in a future article.

Private: A private room is not listed in the public chats. The only way to enter it is to know the name of the private room up front. Private rooms can be shared via Pings. If you are a player's owner, or lover, you can see the name of the room they are in from your friends list.

Locked: A locked room can only be entered or exited by an Administrator. Other players cannot join the room, and players already in the room that are not room administrators cannot leave.
