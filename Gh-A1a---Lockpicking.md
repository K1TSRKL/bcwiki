# Lockpicking

For two of the club's locks, (the "intricate" and "high security" locks), mostly the way the lock gets opened is for somebody to pick the lock.  

That's not the _only_ way to open the locks.  Like most of the club's locks, the usual three people "have a key", and can open the lock without needing to pick the lock.  

* The person who applied the lock.
* The owner (if any) of the person it's locked on.  
* The lover(s) (if any) of the person it's locked on.  

(The "story" for the last two groups is that the owner/lover's key acts as a "master key", but only when used on one's lovers/subs.  This also means that to unlock the lock, the owner/lover has to own the appropriate key.)  

But for _almost_ everybody, to get somebody out of these locks, you'll have to pick the lock.  

## Lock Picks

You'll have to have lock picks.  

You can buy lock picks in the club store.  They're one of the four items that I recommended every new person to buy, as soon as they can.  Like everything in the store, once you buy them, you own an infinite quantity of them.  (Unless you are a fully collared submissive.  In which case, your owner _does_ have the power to take them away from you.)  

And someone who owns lock picks can "loan" picks to somebody who doesn't have them.  (It's under the "Character Actions" menu.  

"Loaned" lockpicks can only be used a few times, and then the game will say that "you broke your lock pick".  At which time, the person who owns them can "loan" you another one.  

Other than the limited number of uses, "loaned" lock picks act just like ones you buy.  But you'll really want to buy them.
