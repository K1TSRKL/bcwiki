# The "Manage my relarionship" lists.  

If you click on another player, one of the buttons you will see next to them is "Manage my relationship".  If you click on that button, it will give you the options to place that person on one of four lists.  

1.  Ghost
2.  Black
3.  White
4.  Friends

These lists are exclusive to you.  They are _your_ lists.  

**None of these lists are permanent.**  You can take people off them, too.  For example, if you click to put somebody on your whitelist, then the next time you go to "manage my relationship" with them, the "add to whitelist" button will be a "Remove from whitelist" button.  

**None of these lists notifies _the other person_ that you've done it.**  The only way somebody can tell that you've put them on a list, is by the effects that it might cause.  

**The game will notify _you_ that a person is on one of your lists.**  It will put an icon above the person to tell you that "this person is on your ____ list".  (A different icon for each list.)  But you are the only person who sees these icons.  If you see that person again, later, it will put that icon above them.  

**Some of the lists _do_ affect other lists.**  

* The black and white lists are exclusive.  If you put somebody on your blacklist, then you can't put them on your whitelist, unless you remove them from blacklist first.  And vice versa.  
* Adding somebody to your ghost list automatically puts them on your blacklist, too.  

## What the lists do

### Blacklist

In almost all cases (the only exception I can think of, is people who have chosen to use the "Everybody, no exceptions" permission setting), putting somebody on your blacklist cuts them off from all ability to touch you.  

And that's _all_ it does.  

Blacklist somebody, and the game doesn't even tell them that you did it.  All that happens is, that person can't do things to you any more.  It's actually hard for someone to figure out that they've been blacklisted.  

It's all or nothing.  Blacklist somebody, and they can't put restraints on you.  But they also can't pet your head.  Or brush your hair.  In fact, if you're tied up, they can't untie you.  

### Ghost

Ghost works similar to blacklist.  In fact, putting somebody on "ghost" automatically adds them to you blacklist.  

Ghost also suppresses all messages from that person.  

Ghost somebody, and if they speak, you won't see the message on your system.  If they whisper to you, you won't even get told about it.  

_I recommend against using ghost on people_.  I did it once.  Regretted it.  Un-ghosted the person.  Never used it since.  

The person was a troll.  A disposable character some folks would create, so they could run through the club and just tie people up, without consent.  They used to be a common nuisance in the club, but they're pretty rare now days.  

But this troll, after tying up everybody in the room, would stand around and lecture everybody.  Racist stuff, as I remember.  

So I put her on ghost.  Didn't have to read her rant, any more.  

Several hours later, the troll came back.  

I wasn't worried.  I had ghosted her.  I was immune.  I ignored her.  

But then I noticed that all my friends had restraints on them, that they didn't have before.  

The troll had attacked everybody else in the room.  She'd left me alone, because she couldn't touch me.  But attacked everybody else.  

And the ghost list had hidden all the restraint messages from me.  

I un-ghosted her.  And haven't used it since.  Now days, if somebody who's on my blacklist comes into a room, I want to get told if they do anything.  

### Whitelist

Putting people on your whitelist _can_ give them more access to do things to you, than if they weren't on whitelist.  

* If you're just strictly on the default new player settings - on "Owner, lover, whitelist, and dominants" - then people who don't have a "dominant" reputation can't do things like tie you up.  But if you put them on your whitelist, then they can.  

* If you've chosen to use the "owner, lover, whitelist" setting, it's a similar thing.  Before they can tie (or untie) you, you have to add them to your whitelist.  

* If you're on the "everybody but blacklist" setting that most people use, then you _can_ designate some game items, that normal people can't use, but people on your whitelist can.  But that's only if you've done the work of designating such items.  By default, whitelist would have no effect.  

Really, how much whitelist affects a person, will depend on other options you've set.  

A lot of things, "whitelist" means "bypass all restrictions, this person is trusted".  But that's not what it means here in the club.  Yes, people on whitelist can still have restrictions on them.  

### Friend list

Adding somebody to your friend list does not require the other person's permission.  But, it also doesn't do much, unless the other person adds you to their friend list, too.  

Adding somebody to your friend list puts a "friend" icon above their avatar.  And that's all it does.  

_Unless the other person adds you to their friend list, too._ 

**If two people friend each other**, then the game's "friend finder" list will show you when your friend is online.  And if she's in a chat room, it will tell you which room.  Also, the "big list of rooms" will highlight the rooms that have friends of yours inside.  

And, two people who have friended each other, and are both online, can "beep" each other, from the friend finder screen.  (I intend to add more on that ability, later.)
